DISCOUNT POLICY

1. Discounts are offered to improve sales volume and support customer promotions.

2. Standard discounts may vary by product category and customer segment.

3. Orders with a discount greater than 20 percent require additional review.

4. Furniture orders with a discount greater than 30 percent require manager approval.

5. High discounts should be reviewed to ensure that revenue and profit are not negatively affected.

6. Corporate and Consumer customers may receive promotional discounts based on business campaigns.
